Frequently asked questions

FAQ

A few quick answers about the Employer Lead Generation Program.

Who is the Employer Lead Generation Program for?

It is designed for immigration firms looking to connect with qualified Canadian employers who are hiring and open to discussing work permit and PR pathways.

What makes an employer introduction qualified?

We identify employers as a fit before making an introduction. The program focuses on employers with open roles who are open to exploring immigration pathways with your firm's guidance.

Which regions does the program cover?

The program covers the Lower Mainland and all three BC RCIP regions.

How many employer introductions are included?

The monthly package offers up to 1–3 qualified employer introductions. The annual partnership offers up to 36 qualified employer leads over 12 months. These are maximums, not guarantees.

Does West Ventures guarantee a job, work permit, or PR outcome?

No. West Ventures does not guarantee immigration outcomes. Employer introductions do not guarantee employment or an immigration result.

How do I learn about pricing and get started?

Pricing is available by inquiry. Book a free demo to learn about the program and discuss the packages.
